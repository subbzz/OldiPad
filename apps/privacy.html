Privacy

This is a personal YouTube app for one old iPad. It uses YouTube API Services.

Your Google sign-in, watch history, Watch later list, and settings are stored only on your iPad, in this app's storage. Nothing is sent to the app's author or any server other than Google's.

The app asks Google for permission to manage your YouTube account so you can like videos, subscribe, comment, and save to playlists. You can remove that permission at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by tapping Sign out in the app.

By using this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
